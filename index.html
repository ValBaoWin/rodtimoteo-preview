Rod built his name one chair at a time — from humble beginnings to founding Mystérieux, a contemporary barbering space and fashion-forward lifestyle brand in the heart of downtown San Jose.

Behind the clippers he's a teacher of fundamentals, systems and customer experience, a photographer on his Sony A7iii, and a full-time dog dad to Biggie the Frenchie.
